# Blog posts and guides

Start with the question a customer would type into Google or ask on the phone.
The post exists to answer it better than anyone else, using what the owner
actually knows.

## Shape

1. **Title:** the question or the answer, in the customer's words. "Why does my
   neck hurt more on Fridays?" beats "Understanding Cervical Strain".
2. **Opening:** answer it in the first two or three sentences. Don't warm up
   with industry background.
3. **Body:** headings that each state a point or a step. Read the headings
   alone and they should make sense in order. Under each, explain it the way
   the owner did, with their example.
4. **Ending:** stop when the question is answered. Add what to do next: book,
   call, read another post. One ask.

Most business posts land at 600 to 1,200 words. Write what the material
supports; don't pad to a number.

## Turning a video or call into a post

Pick one question from the recording and build the post around it. Don't
summarise the whole recording in order. Keep their examples and explanations;
add headings and tidy the spoken stumbles.

## A story or opinion post

One real decision or event they told you about, what they were weighing up and
what happened. It's fine if the ending is "we're still working this out".
Don't add a dramatic low point or a neat lesson they never said.

## Hand over

Title, a one-line meta description (under about 155 characters), the post, and
in the notes: sources, any image you'd suggest, and anything to check.
