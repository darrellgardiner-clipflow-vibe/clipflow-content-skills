# Hooks

The hook is the first line spoken and the first text on screen. It decides
whether anyone hears the rest. It has to make sense to someone who has never
heard of this business, and it has to be true.

## Kinds that work for a business

Write three hooks of different kinds and pick the one most specific to this
owner and this post. The examples are for a made-up physio clinic.

| Kind | Example |
|---|---|
| Their customer's question, in the customer's words | "Why does my neck hurt more on Fridays?" |
| A specific moment | "A patient came in on Tuesday carrying his laptop bag like it weighed a tonne." |
| A number they actually said | "Most of the desk workers we see on Fridays sit for nine hours a day." |
| A mistake the viewer is probably making | "If you stretch your neck every time it aches at your desk, you're making Friday worse." |
| Who it's for, named exactly | "If you work at a desk and your neck is worst by the end of the week, this is for you." |
| Their opinion against the usual advice | "Standing desks didn't fix a single one of our patients' necks. Here's what did." |

## Rules

- Name the person or the situation. "If you run a café..." beats "Business owners..."
- Use their words and their customers' words, not marketing words.
- Spoken hooks land in about three seconds, roughly ten words. On-screen
  hooks are eight words or fewer.
- Only promise what the post delivers. If the hook says "here's what fixed it",
  the post has to say what fixed it.
- No number unless they said it. No result unless it happened.

## Openers to avoid

They're what every account opens with, so viewers scroll past them:
"In this video...", "Did you know...", "Hey guys, so today...", "Ever wondered...",
"Let's talk about...", "Here's the thing", "Stop scrolling", "POV:" unless it's
real and specific, and any opener built as "It's not X, it's Y".

## When a clip's best line is in the middle

Put that line on screen as the hook for the first two or three seconds, and
start the clip just before it, or open with a three-second excerpt of it and
then cut to the start.
