# Resolve a piece brief

Put these decisions in the existing piece record. A short edit needs only the
relevant fields; do not invent values to complete a template.

```text
Mode and requested deliverables:
Business / author / account / platform / format / series:
Reader and situation:
Reader's question:
Supported answer and its boundary:
Business purpose and desired response, or explicit none:
Preserve list and allowed edit scope:
Evidence IDs and source locations; missing or changing facts:
Selected angle and reason; alternatives if consequential:
Pattern or free structure:
Outline: each section/slide/beat's job and supporting evidence:
Voice samples and other selected rules, with their scope:
Overrides: inherited instruction, replacement, reason and authorization:
Available visuals and proposed capture/render dependencies:
Length/duration target and basis (estimate or verified limit):
CTA, destination, offer/asset availability:
Output fields and artifact path:
Review status, exact revision and unresolved items:
Optional primary success measure, denominator and observation window:
Requested external action and authorization state:
```

Keep this record outside public copy. Its goal is to preserve the decisions a
writer and reviewer need, without attaching the entire brand archive to each piece.
