# Review the final piece and retain useful feedback

Read the saved draft, resolved brief, selected brand rules and original supporting
passages. Review the actual final revision, not an earlier response or a mental
summary. A finding needs a location, reason and practical repair.

## Review in order

1. **Meaning and grounding:** check consequential facts, speaker ownership,
   attribution, dates, qualifications and current offers. Trace source IDs back
   to passages; a citation must support this claim, not merely share a keyword.
2. **Reader and argument:** does the piece answer the chosen question, and does
   the body deliver the opening's promise? Remove interesting material belonging
   in another piece. Revisit the angle if the only way to support it is to qualify
   it beyond usefulness.
3. **Structure and format:** read headings, slide headlines or beats in sequence.
   Confirm each advances the explanation and that requested fields are distinct.
   Apply the selected format's own checks rather than a universal paragraph rule.
4. **Voice and language:** compare with the author's relevant samples, preserve
   deliberate wording and apply the business's actual style requirements.
5. **Production and action:** inspect asset dependencies, attribution, destination,
   timing assumptions and promised deliverables. Copy readiness, visual readiness
   and publication authorization are separate states.

## Mechanical pass, then semantic read

Check exact requirements mechanically when possible: supplied forbidden terms,
names, requested fields, links, unresolved placeholders, repeated text and length
budgets. Review every match in context. Use the business's supplied rules, not an
unexplained inherited blacklist. Do not damage Unicode, emoji or URLs in cleanup.

Then read the whole piece for problems exact matching misses: disconnected
reasoning, repeated conclusions, generic preambles, rehearsed contrast slogans,
abstract claims without examples, and phrasing that changes what the author meant.
Join thoughts when sentence breaks make an argument choppy; vary lengths for
readability without forcing a numerical cadence target. Preserve honest
uncertainty, personal phrasing and a purposeful CTA. Familiar words are not proof
that a text was generated by AI.

## Revise at the correct level

If the draft is accurate but irrelevant, revisit the reader and angle. If it sounds
wrong, revisit voice evidence and edit scope. If the pattern needs invention,
change the pattern. If revisions repeat the same origin story, select a more
relevant belief, question or source. If feedback conflicts, resolve its scope and
authority before editing again. A short comment does not need to become an essay
to satisfy a review heuristic.

Record the exact final revision, checks performed, unresolved items and overall
state. A promised asset that is unavailable yields `draft complete; fulfilment
blocked`. Missing required product verification holds the affected instructions.
Do not bury these holds beneath a headline saying ready.

## Learn from corrections and outcomes

Record an approved correction as original line, revised line, reason, author,
format/scope and date. Distinguish the owner's explanation from your inference.
One edit can guide the next similar piece without becoming a universal rule.
Repeated relevant feedback can justify a proposed profile update; preserve its
evidence and obtain the authority needed to adopt it.

Performance review needs actual results, raw counts, denominators and a comparable
window. Separate observed outcomes, possible explanations and the next test. A
post's popularity cannot establish the truth of its claims or prove a hook caused
the result. Keep writing quality, production quality and business outcomes distinct.
