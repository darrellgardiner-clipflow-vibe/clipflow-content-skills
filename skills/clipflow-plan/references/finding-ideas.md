# Finding post ideas in a recording

Most owners have said dozens of good posts out loud without noticing. Read the
transcript slowly and look for these. Each line below says what to listen for.

| Kind | Listen for | Becomes |
|---|---|---|
| Customer question | "People always ask", "the number one question", a caller's or commenter's question | A post that answers it straight, in the first few seconds |
| Mistake | "The biggest mistake I see", "people come in after they've already..." | "If you're doing X, stop. Here's what happens." |
| Story with a turn | A specific client, job or day where something went wrong or changed | The story, told from the moment it turns |
| Disagreement | "Everyone says X, but", "I hate when", "unpopular opinion" | Their opinion and the reason behind it |
| How it's done | Steps, tools, a process, what a first visit looks like | A carousel or a "what happens when you book" video |
| Specific number | Prices, timeframes, counts, "nine out of ten" | A hook built on the number, only if they said it |
| Myth | "People think", "it's not true that" | Myth, the truth, and how they know |
| Before and after | A change they describe in a client, a space, a result | A before and after post, if they have the visuals |
| Buying question | Price, how long it takes, what to expect, how to choose someone | The posts that bring enquiries, so find these first |

## What makes a post bring enquiries

For a small business, the posts that bring messages and bookings usually:

- answer a question someone asks when they're close to buying, such as price,
  timing, process, what to expect, or how to pick someone;
- speak to a specific person in a specific situation ("if you're a desk worker
  whose neck is worst on Fridays"), rather than everyone;
- show the actual owner or team, so people feel they've met them;
- end with one clear, easy next step, said exactly ("Book a Thursday
  assessment, the link's in our bio", or "DM me the word QUOTE");
- repeat. The same offer, asked for in different posts over weeks, does more
  than a single clever one.

Generic tips anyone in the industry could post rarely bring enquiries. Neither
do trends that don't connect to what they sell. Prefer the post only this
business could make.

## Is this moment clippable?

A moment from a recording works as a post on its own when:

1. It starts at the point, not the wind-up. Cut "So, yeah, um, I wanted to talk
   about" and start where the real sentence starts.
2. It makes sense with no context. No "like I said earlier", no "this guy" without
   saying who.
3. It's one idea, 20 to 60 seconds long.
4. It ends on the payoff or a natural stop, not mid-thought.

Give the start and end as the transcript times where those sentences begin and
end, so the trim is easy to find. If the strongest line is in the middle, note
it as the on-screen hook and suggest starting the clip just before it.

## Writing ideas as hooks

Every idea in `ideas.md` is written as the first line a viewer would hear or
read, in the owner's words where possible. "Neck pain tips" is a topic and isn't
usable. "If your neck is worst by Friday, count how long you sat this week" is
a post.
