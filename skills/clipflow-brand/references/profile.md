# A working profile for a business or a founder

Keep this profile for the account the owner named. Write in plain language
and tag each usable observation to its passage in `content/sources.md`.
Use the owner's approved wording where supplied. Distinguish what a page
says, what the owner confirmed, and what you infer. Record a gap in one line
when a document has little evidence.

If the request covers both a company and its founder, keep a profile folder
for each inside `content/brand/` and label plan entries with the publishing
account. Link shared offer facts. Keep personal stories and voice examples
with their speaker.

| File under `content/brand/` | What a writer needs |
|---|---|
| `overview.md` | The business or person, what they do, who publishes on this account and the goal for this week. Separate a founder's experience from the company's track record. |
| `audience.md` | The likely buyer, who uses the offer, the situation that brings them here, the question they need answered and what they can do next. Say which segment the page addresses and why. A service page shows who the offer is *for*. Actual customers, demographics and objections need evidence of their own. Mark a proposed ideal customer profile as an inference. |
| `voice.md` | Short examples in the relevant speaker's words and how those examples should shape a draft. Separate company copy, named authors and guest quotes. With only a services page, describe the site's written tone and leave the founder's spoken voice open. |
| `offers-and-proof.md` | What is available now, the action and destination, and the example or passage behind each public claim. Keep old prices, future plans and unconfirmed results out of current offers. Note conflicts for the owner. |
| `design-language.md` | Visual choices you could actually see, with the source image or page location. Distinguish what appears in one example from a repeated style. With text alone, record that appearance is unknown. |

Put a source and a date beside a correction. Preserve approved voice, goals
and owner edits on refresh. A new site headline can update positioning. It
cannot change a founder's biography or turn a buyer hypothesis into a result.
