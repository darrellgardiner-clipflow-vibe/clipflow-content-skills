# File-based brand records

Reuse an existing structure. For a new brand workspace, `brand.md` can hold the
maintained sections and links to source notes and claims. Split out notes only
when their volume makes this easier to use. Never overwrite original sources or
unrelated human edits. These are suggested fields, not a database schema.

## Source note

```text
Source ID and title/path or URL:
Published / observed date, author and source version:
Evidence form and scope reviewed:
Meaning and useful reader questions:
Passages: exact quote + actual line, timestamp, slide or asset location
Observations and their supporting passages:
Candidate claims and evidence family:
Unreviewed material, uncertainty and next check:
```

## Claim

```text
Claim ID / type:
Statement with qualifiers:
Original source IDs and exact passages:
Authority: owner direction / direct account / attributed third party / inference
Scope: author, audience, format/platform, conditions and time period
Evidence family (underlying event or statement):
Assessment: usable in this scope / provisional / historical / contested / superseded
Reason, missing verification and date assessed:
```

A source can contain a true quotation of an unverified outcome. Preserve both:
the person said it, and the outcome has not been independently established.
Do not upgrade a candidate claim to owner-approved because it passed a structural
check. A style preference can be explicit without being a proven growth tactic.

## Working brand knowledge

```text
Business / author / account and requested use:
Overview and positioning:
Audience: current, desired, buyer/user distinctions, situations and questions
Voice: observable choice + short example + source + scope/approval standing
Offers and proof: terms, availability, evidence, conditions and historical items
Beliefs/mechanisms: question, reasoning, trade-off, limits and relevant receipts
Design language: inspected evidence and observation limits, if relevant
Coverage: sources reviewed, sampling basis, missing forms and uncertain identities
Open conflicts or missing inputs:
Changes: date, affected statement, previous interpretation and reason
```

Keep a revision or source-version marker where practical. If a source or directive
changes, mark affected conclusions for review. A draft generated from this brief
is output, not new independent evidence that the brief is correct.
