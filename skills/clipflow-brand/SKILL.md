# Write the brand brief

The brief is what lets every later post sound like this owner and sell what
they actually sell. It is a working page for writing posts, not a brand
strategy deck. One page, specific lines, every line traceable to their material.

Get the material in first with
[getting material](../clipflow-content/references/getting-material.md). Use the
template and source tags in [files](../clipflow-content/references/files.md),
and follow the [ground rules](../clipflow-content/references/ground-rules.md).

## 1. Read everything once, all the way through

For links, start with the [public-link intake](../clipflow-content/references/getting-material.md#public-links).
Identify the business, speaker and publishing account. A company homepage can
establish its offer. A bylined post can establish what that person said.
Read the relevant pages, transcripts and posts before writing. Note with
source tags and a section, passage or timestamp:

- **Customer questions.** Any time they say "people always ask", "the question
  I get", "a lot of clients think", or answer a question from a caller or
  commenter. These are the most valuable lines in the whole pile.
- **Stories.** A specific client, job, day or mistake, with what happened and
  what changed. Note where it starts and ends.
- **Opinions.** Where they disagree with common advice or other people in their
  industry, and their reason.
- **Specifics.** Prices, timeframes, numbers, places, tools, steps in how they
  work. Specifics make posts believable.
- **Proof.** Results, reviews, years in business, qualifications, but only what
  they actually said or showed.
- **The offer.** What they sell, what it costs if mentioned, how people buy or
  book, what they want more of.
- **Voice.** Lines that sound unmistakably like them.

## 2. Describe how they talk, with examples

Voice notes are only useful if a writer can copy them. Write what they do, and
quote them doing it:

- Weak: "Friendly and authentic."
- Useful: "Opens with a client situation before the point: 'Had a bloke in on
  Tuesday who'd been sleeping on the couch for a week.' [S1 03:12]"
- Useful: "Says 'mate' and 'heaps'; never uses anatomy terms without
  explaining them straight after. [S1, S2]"

Note how they open, how they explain, how formal they are, whether they swear
or joke, and any phrases they repeat. Add the words and styles they'd never
use if it's clear (corporate words, hype, emoji). Don't invent quirks, and
don't read a personality into punctuation.

With only one or two samples, say the voice section is a first guess.

## 3. Write the brief

Fill [the brief template](../clipflow-content/references/files.md) in
`content/brief.md`. Keep each section to the few lines that a writer would
actually use. Put customer questions in the customer's words.

For a weekly plan, a few tagged lines on the reader, offer, voice and usable
proof belong in the brief. Link any existing brand files that already cover
them. When the owner asks for a full brand profile, use [the five brand
documents](references/profile.md) and link them from the brief. If they only
ask for their ideal customer profile, write the audience document. A single
caption request can go straight to writing.

Write down the gaps honestly: no reviews supplied, no prices mentioned, only
one recording. For each gap, write a question they could answer on camera in a
minute to fill it. Those questions feed the plan's "Next time you film" list.

## 4. Updating an existing brief

When new material arrives, add what's new and change only what it changes.
Put the date and source next to each change. A page fetched today can still
describe an old offer. When sources conflict, use an explicitly current,
owner-confirmed detail or leave the disputed claim out and ask if it matters
to this week. Preserve the owner's corrections.

## Hand back

If this was the only step requested, tell them in a few lines: who you think
the customer is, the two or three strongest things you found, and the biggest
gap. Ask them to correct anything wrong; their corrections go straight into
the brief. Otherwise continue to [plan](../clipflow-plan/SKILL.md) or
[write](../clipflow-write/SKILL.md) as requested.
