# Brand Lens: understand the business through its sources

Build knowledge that helps a writer answer customers' questions in this business's
voice. Start with the existing brief and the current request; determine what needs
to be learned rather than reprocessing every source for every piece.

Read [extraction and refresh](references/extraction.md) for the source-to-knowledge
method and [the shared handoff](../clipflow-content/references/handoff.md) for the
working fields. Use [sources](../clipflow-content/references/sources.md) for input
handling. No separate Brand Lens application or database is required.

## Work from the available material

1. Resolve the business, author, audience and content job. Inventory the actual
   source forms and review coverage: transcript, caption, slides, visuals or owner
   instruction. Keep different clients and speakers distinct.
2. Read for meaning, grounding and relevance. Write focused source notes with
   exact passages and locations. Extract candidate claims, useful questions,
   mechanisms, stories and observable writing/design choices.
3. Assess each useful claim's authority, scope, currentness and independence.
   Separate supplied instructions, observations, inference and historical material.
   Reposts of one event are one evidence family, not several independent proofs.
4. Compile overview/positioning, audience, voice and offers/proof. Add design
   language only from inspected visual evidence. Link conclusions to claims or
   source passages and retain gaps and conflicting interpretations.
5. Capture how the author reasons: the situation, question, trade-off, explanation
   and conditions behind a view. Use those principles for new questions, marking
   extrapolations as proposed rather than inventing an opinion.
6. Save the relevant knowledge in the existing project, with source coverage and
   dated changes. On refresh, update affected claims and documents while retaining
   corrections and prior reasoning. Use [templates](references/records.md) when a
   new record is needed; a small brief can keep these fields together.

Voice is learned from relevant examples and approved edits. Format mechanics
such as slide counts, caption footers or video length remain in account/format
instructions. Keep the owner's intent distinct from a habit inferred from samples.

Output the working brand brief and its grounded notes/claims, coverage, conflicts
and material open questions. Continue to [planning](../clipflow-plan/SKILL.md) or
[writing](../clipflow-write/SKILL.md) when requested. Do not require a service login,
fixed sample count or complete archive before producing useful scoped work.
