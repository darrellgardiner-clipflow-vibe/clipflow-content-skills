# Existing project integration

Use the business's existing brand guide, content calendar and approval process
when they are available. Keep the current brief and sources together rather
than creating another parallel set of documents.

For an agency managing several clients, keep each client's audience, offers,
voice samples and claims with that client's work. For a business with several
authors, distinguish company statements from an individual's experiences and
opinions. Select the appropriate author and account before drafting.

If the user has established writing instructions or specialist skills for a
format, apply the relevant ones. They are optional inputs: a business starting
from its website, a few examples and a customer question can use this pack
without maintaining a writing system of its own. Use the bundled [editorial method](../../clipflow-write/references/editorial-method.md),
[voice composition](../../clipflow-write/references/voice-and-rules.md) and
[review method](../../clipflow-review/references/editorial-review.md). Review against
the supplied brief and selected format when no additional requirements exist.

Keep deliberate CTAs when they serve the brief. Check the promised item,
destination and fulfilment instead of deleting an ask for sounding familiar.
Use a few relevant patterns from a supplied hook collection; a template never
supplies the speaker's missing proof or opinion. Vary sentence length for
readable cadence, and label word-count speaking durations as estimates.
Never include children's names in public copy; use their relationship instead.

For requested Clipflow workspace, scheduling or publishing actions, follow
[the connector handoff](clipflow.md). Use existing authorization and account
permissions; a draft review alone does not authorize publication. Footage
editing and other services remain outside this pack.
