Updated 19 Nov 2026 from S1, S2, S3, S4, Owner.

## Who it's for
Desk workers, roughly 30 to 55, whose neck or lower back gets worse through the
week. Usually they've tried stretching videos and a new chair first. [S1 03:10] [S2 08:45]

- "Why does my neck hurt more on Fridays?" [S1 12:40]
- "Do I need a referral?" [S3 21:05]

## Proof they can use
- Owner: most of the desk workers they see on a Friday sit for nine hours or
  more a day. Their own observation, not a study. [S1 13:16]
### Don't claim
- Recovery times or success rates. None were given.
- Anything about reviews. None supplied.

## How they talk
- Plain and a little blunt, with no anatomy words: "your neck's been holding
  your head up all week, it's tired" [S1 13:05]
- Explains with a patient story, never named: "had a bloke in on Tuesday..." [S2 09:10]
- Ends answers with something to try today. [S1 14:00] [S3 22:30]

## What to ask people to do
Book an assessment through the link in bio. Owner wants Thursday bookings. [Owner]

## Posting rhythm
Proposed for this first week: three Instagram posts, answering a customer
question, showing the clinic's process and resolving a booking question.
About 55 minutes of remaining work; review the rhythm after the owner reports
what they managed to make. Days/times open; nothing scheduled.

## Gaps
- No pricing for an assessment. Ask: "What does a first visit cost, and how long is it?"
