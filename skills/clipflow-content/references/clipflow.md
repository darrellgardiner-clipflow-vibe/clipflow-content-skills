# Optional: save and schedule in Clipflow

Everything in this pack works without Clipflow. When the owner asks to put the
plan or finished posts into Clipflow, schedule them or publish them, use the
tools their connected Clipflow account exposes. This pack contains no
connection, login or keys of its own.

## Find the right place

Look at which Clipflow tools are actually available and read their inputs.
Use read-only lookups to find the right organization, project and channel.
Don't guess an ID, a tool name or a permission, and don't assume a similar-looking
account is the right one. If they're not connected, finish the local work and
tell them how to connect; never ask for a password.

## Do only what they asked

Creating a draft post, scheduling it and publishing it are three different
actions. Do the one they asked for. Before each one:

- Use the reviewed version of the copy and the right media.
- Confirm the channel, date, time and time zone for anything scheduled.
- Hold anything with an open "check before posting" item.
- Don't touch other posts in their calendar.

Write the returned post ID and its actual status into the post's notes. Check
the status with a lookup tool when one exists; a "created" reply doesn't mean
it's live. If a call times out, check what happened before trying again so a
post isn't published twice.
