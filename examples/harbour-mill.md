# Fictional business: Harbour Mill

All people, events and business details here are invented for exercising the skills.
No external research or connected account is needed.

## Request

Understand the brand, plan two pieces from these sources, and draft a short spoken
video plus a carousel with a separate caption. The audience is homeowners comparing
custom cupboards. One hour is available to review and record; existing workshop
photos may be suggested, but none have been supplied for visual inspection.
Write as founder Morgan. Preserve this CTA: "Reply PLAN for the measuring guide."
The guide is unfinished. No publication or scheduling is requested.

## S1: founder interview, 12 November 2026

Morgan: "I ask what gets put away first. If someone needs a place for a vacuum,
I want to know that before we draw shelves. On our own workshop cupboard we made
a cardboard model and noticed the door would hit a bench. We moved the hinge in
the drawing. I cannot tell you how much rework that prevented; we did not measure it."

Morgan: "Customers often bring a picture they love. We talk about what has to fit
inside it and how the doors will open in their room. I like plain explanations.
I don't tell people that custom work is always the right answer."

Guest consultant: "A cardboard model doubles everyone's productivity."
Morgan: "I wouldn't promise that. We use it to notice problems before cutting."
