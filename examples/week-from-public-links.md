# A week from two published articles

Ken Greeff has already written both pieces on Clipflow's blog. This example
proposes a week for his personal LinkedIn account. The time budget, days and
draft below are illustrative. The links and the ideas in the articles are real.

## The proposed week

**Make Tuesday's post first.** Open the draft below and read it aloud. It
needs only a wording check. Thursday needs a new recording.

| Day | Post for LinkedIn | Source | Remaining work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tuesday | **Text post.** Which customer question have you answered twice? Give the reader one place to look for an idea today. | S1, closing paragraph | Read the draft in Ken's voice, make any edits and prepare the post. About 15 minutes. |
| Thursday | **Short phone video.** How many posts could you still make on a busy week? Explain how to choose a pace, batch the work and review which topics started conversations. | S2, pacing and batching passages | Outline, record one take, trim, caption and check it. About 40 minutes. |

Estimated work is **55 minutes of the available hour**. The articles supply
both ideas. The video still needs filming. If the week gets busy, finish the
text post and carry the video forward.

## Tuesday's draft

Open your sent emails. Which customer question have you answered twice?

The second person who asked couldn't see the answer you sent the first.
Someone else is probably trying to work it out right now. Write down the
question and talk them through the answer you'd usually give in private.

Leave the customer's details out. The answer is the part worth sharing.
