# One website link and 35 minutes for LinkedIn

In a test run on 20 November 2026, we gave the skills a Clipflow service page
and asked for a week on the company LinkedIn account. The posts below were
proposals. The time estimates cover the owner's remaining work.

```text
Use clipflow-content with this page from our company website:
https://www.clipflow.co/features/video-review
We've got 35 minutes for LinkedIn next week. What should we post? Draft the first one.
```

The agent opened the supplied page and followed links to the client sharing
page and agency FAQ. It used company copy for product facts. The customer
testimonials belonged to their speakers.

## The week of 23 November

| Day | Suggested post | Where it came from | What remains |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tuesday | Does a client need an account to sign off on a video? | The guest review FAQ in [Video Review & Approval](https://www.clipflow.co/features/video-review) and the [client sharing page](https://www.clipflow.co/features/client-review) | Review the written draft, preview its link and post. About 7 minutes. |
| Thursday | Which cut goes into the draft post after approval? | The “Approved to published” section of the video review page | Write a short explanation, check the steps and post. About 18 minutes. |
| Friday | Check what actually went out | The two entries above | Note which posts got made and how long they took. About 2 minutes. |

**Make Tuesday's post first.** Copy the draft into the company LinkedIn composer
and preview the link. The plan uses about 27 of the available 35 minutes.
Neither post needs filming.

## From Tuesday's draft

> Does your client need a Clipflow account to sign off on a video?
>
> A share link is enough. Pick the cut you want them to see and send it over.
> Your client can watch, leave a timestamped note or approve the edit right there.

The question came from Clipflow's own FAQ. The [agency FAQ](https://www.clipflow.co/solutions/agencies)
also describes timestamped comments. The draft links to the client sharing
page so a reader can see how the link works.

## Try it with your site

Replace the URL, publishing account and available time in the request. If the
agent can't read the page, paste the relevant paragraph or a caption. One
readable buying question and answer can be enough for a first post.
